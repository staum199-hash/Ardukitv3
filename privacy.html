Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Electronics Toolkit (“the site”) handles when you use it at https://ardukit.netlify.app.

Information we do not collect

The site has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that send personal data to us. The calculators and designers run inside your browser, and what you enter into them is not uploaded to our servers.

Data stored on your device

To make the tools convenient, the site saves a few settings in your browser’s local storage: your chosen language and color theme, the selected board, your matrix drawing and animation frames, and your project-wizard selection. This data never leaves your device and you can remove it at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser. If you install the site as an app, a service worker also keeps a copy of the pages on your device so they work offline.

Advertising and cookies

The guide pages show advertisements served by Google AdSense. The tool pages themselves do not show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this site or other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), visitors are asked for their consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Fonts and other third-party resources

The toolkit page loads its typeface from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your IP address and standard browser request information. The guide pages use your device’s own fonts.

Server logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and operation of the service.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the contact page.
