About Electronics Toolkit

Electronics Toolkit is a free set of tools for people who build things with microcontrollers. It runs entirely in your browser and works offline once installed on your phone.

What is inside

  • Resistor calculator. Decode 4-band and 5-band color codes and SMD markings, find the color bands for a value, apply Ohm’s law and size an LED resistor.
  • LED color picker and matrix designer. Choose colors, draw on an 8×8 WS2812B matrix, build animations and export ready Arduino code.
  • Arduino tools. A sensor code generator, an ADC calculator, a wiring guide and a project wizard that checks pins for conflicts and risky connections, draws a live board view and lists the parts you need.
  • Guides. Plain-language articles that explain the ideas behind the tools.

Why it exists

Many small electronics mistakes come from the same few things: a misread resistor, an LED without enough current limiting, a pin that is not suited to the job. The toolkit tries to make those checks quick and visible, and to produce code you can paste straight into the Arduino IDE.

Privacy first

The tools do their work on your device. Your drawings, pin choices and settings stay in your browser. See the privacy policy for details, including how advertising is handled on the guide pages.

Accuracy and responsibility

We take care to keep the calculations and guidance correct, but electronics hardware varies. Component values, pinouts and limits differ between manufacturers and board versions. Always check the datasheet of your own parts before connecting power, and use appropriate protection when working with higher currents or voltages. The tools and guides are provided as they are, without warranty.
